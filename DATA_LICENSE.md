## Dataset material

The datasets are collected from the [Gray Zone Warfare Wiki on Fandom](https://gray-zone-warfare.fandom.com/wiki/Gray_Zone_Warfare_Wiki). Fandom's [licensing guidance](https://community.fandom.com/wiki/Help:Licensing) says wiki text is generally offered under [CC BY-SA 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/) unless noted otherwise. The GZW wiki also displays a general CC-BY-SA notice.

That general notice does not establish that every structured fact, game-related element, image, or other asset in this repository has the same license. Some material may have separate rights or exceptions. This repository does **not** claim that all API data is uniformly licensed under CC BY-SA or MIT, and it does not grant rights that its maintainers do not own. No independent legal determination of every record or asset has been completed.

The data files store image URLs rather than copies of the images. Linked images are served from their source hosts and remain subject to the rights and terms applicable to those assets.

## Attribution and provenance

When reusing wiki material that is covered by CC BY-SA, follow the license terms: identify and link the license, provide attribution to the source article (or an equivalent source record), and indicate modifications. The API exposes dataset-level source-page titles and revision IDs when available; these are provenance hints, not a complete per-record author history.
